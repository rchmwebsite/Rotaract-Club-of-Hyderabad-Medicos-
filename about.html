Who We Are

The Rotaract Club of Hyderabad Medicos is a student-led organization comprising medical and allied health sciences students united by a commitment to service, leadership, and social responsibility.

What We Do

We organize health awareness programs, blood donation drives, medical camps, and social outreach initiatives while fostering leadership, teamwork, and ethical values among our members.

Where We Are

Based in Hyderabad, we serve communities across the city and nearby regions through collaborations with Rotary clubs, medical institutions, and social organizations.
